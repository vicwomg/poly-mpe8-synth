🎹 POLY-MPE // About & Features

About POLY-MPE

POLY-MPE is an expressive 8-voice polyphonic synthesizer engine engineered entirely with the native Web Audio and Web MIDI APIs. It bridges classic analog subtractive synthesis with modern multidimensional MIDI Polyphonic Expression (MPE).

Key Influences

  • Sequential Prophet-5 & Roland Jupiter-8: Dual cross-modulating analog oscillator architecture, rich detuned unison leads, warm poly brass, and resonant 4-pole low-pass filtering.
  • Expressive E Osmose & Roli Seaboard: High-resolution continuous per-note pitch bending (up to ±48 semitones), polyphonic pressure/aftertouch, and seamless note sliding.
  • Americana Pedal Steel & Ambient Guitar: Resonant clean pickup modeling, volume pedal expression swells (CC11), and lush stereo slapback/hall delay networks.

Key Features

  • 8 Polyphonic Voices: Pre-allocated zero-garbage-collection audio graph with round-robin allocation and 2.5ms anti-pop micro-fades.
  • Dual Multi-Wave Oscillators: Sawtooth, Square, Triangle, and Sine waveforms with octave shifting (-2 to +2), semitone transpose, and fine cents detuning.
  • Resonant Low-Pass Filter: 20Hz - 20kHz cutoff with resonance Q, ADSR envelope generator, key tracking, and CC73 cutoff control.
  • MPE Expressive Controls: Continuous per-note pitch bend (±48 semitones), CC11 expression/volume, CC1 mod wheel resonance, and channel aftertouch.
  • Stereo Delay & Studio Limiter: Dual-channel ping-pong stereo delay with feedback and studio soft-clip dynamics compressor.
  • Performance Tools: 2D MPE Touchpad for XY expression testing and on-screen keyboard mapped directly to your computer keyboard.

Playing Controls

Play using the computer keyboard keys (shown directly on the virtual keys: A, W, S, E, D, F...), your mouse/touchscreen, or connect any USB or Bluetooth MIDI/MPE keyboard controller!
